🌸 Spring

  • Test soil pH after winter
  • Apply nitrogen-rich fertilizers
  • Add compost before planting
  • Start seedlings indoors

☀️ Summer

  • Mulch to retain moisture
  • Apply potassium for heat stress
  • Side-dress growing crops
  • Monitor for nutrient deficiency

🍂 Fall

  • Apply phosphorus for root development
  • Plant cover crops
  • Add lime if pH is low
  • Compost fallen leaves

❄️ Winter

  • Let cover crops protect soil
  • Plan next season's rotation
  • Prepare compost bins
  • Review soil test results
